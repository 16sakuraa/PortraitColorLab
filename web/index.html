Skin and photo

Tinted skin is what gets smoothed. Paint the skin area to add skin the mask missed, or take out what shouldn't be smoothed.

Smoothing

The light brush evens out blotches, dark circles and seams between light and shadow with a colour you pick from the skin, Nose line paints a soft highlight down the nose, and Smooth again smooths leftover coarse grain or stubble shadow once more.

Finishing

Bring back sharpness around the eyes, nostrils and jawline with Undo smoothing, cover scars with Heal, and click leftover spots away with Remove spot. Then press Download.

History

Face detection: YuNet (OpenCV Zoo). Skin segmentation and face landmarks: Google MediaPipe selfie multiclass and Face Mesh V2 (Apache 2.0). The optional blemish network (?blemish=network) was trained on FFHQR (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), for non-commercial use.
